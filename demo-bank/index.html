For organisations

Create a key pair, keep the private key on the systems your agents call from, and publish the public key in /.well-known/liveseal.json on your main web address. Issue sub-keys per agent or per team, list a key under revoked the moment a device is lost, and seal every call: a customer who has added you is warned when a call claiming to be you is not sealed.

The file format is in docs/PROTOCOL.md, section 11.

Demo Bank does not exist. Everything on this page runs on your device.
