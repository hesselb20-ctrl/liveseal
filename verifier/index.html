Verify a screen

Works with any call app, including ones on a TV or another computer. Point this device's camera at the picture of the person, or capture the call window.

Drag the four corners onto the corners of the caller's picture. Keep this device still; a stand helps.

Filming a screen is the hardest case for the seal. Expect it to work less often than the browser extension, and treat "not verified" here as "could not read", not as proof of anything.
