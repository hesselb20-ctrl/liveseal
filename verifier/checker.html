What this report can tell you

For each period: whether a seal is present, which pinned key signed it, the time the signer's device claimed, and whether the picture matches what was signed.

A recording cannot prove when it was made. The time shown is the one written into the seal by the signer's device. To check a seal against a particular person, pair with their key first.
