LiveSeal Camera

Adds an invisible seal to your camera picture. People who have paired with you can then check that a call really comes from your device.

First, a name

This is what the people you pair with will see next to "Verified". It stays on this device until you share your pairing code.

Your key is made on this device and never leaves it. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

Camera off

Your picture is sealed on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere by this page.

Someone asks you to verify

They will read you three words. Type them here and keep talking; their screen turns green a few seconds later.

Use it in Zoom, Teams or WhatsApp Desktop

  1. Install the free OBS Studio.
  2. Turn the camera on here and press "Full screen".
  3. In OBS add a "Window Capture" of this window, then press "Start Virtual Camera".
  4. In your call app choose "OBS Virtual Camera" as the camera.

For calls in the browser (Meet, Teams web, Jitsi) the LiveSeal extension does this for you.
